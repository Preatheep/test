Dental Implants in Coimbatore

At Arasu Dental Care, a dental implant gives you back a tooth that looks, bites and feels like your own — a fixed replacement set into your jaw, not something you take out at night. If you've lost a tooth to decay, injury or gum disease, an implant lets you eat, speak and smile normally again, and it protects the jawbone that starts to shrink the moment a tooth goes missing.

It's the right choice for most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth and enough bone to hold the implant — and where bone has thinned, we can usually rebuild it first. Is a dental implant right for you?

You're likely a good candidate if you have one or more missing teeth, healthy gums, and enough jawbone to support an implant. Most healthy adults qualify, and modern planning lets us treat many cases that once would have been turned away.

A few situations need a step first rather than a straight “no.” If the tooth has been missing a while, the bone underneath may have shrunk — a bone graft or sinus lift can rebuild enough support, which we'll know after a 3D scan. Active gum disease is treated before we place an implant, and conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or heavy smoking lower success rates, so we'll talk through them honestly with you.

What we won't do is push an implant on everyone. If a bridge or a denture is the more sensible choice for your case, we'll tell you — see dental crowns & bridges for that route.

What to expect — the implant process

Most single-tooth implants follow four stages, and we explain your exact timeline at the first visit.

  1. 1

    Consultation and 3D scan

    We examine the area and take a digital X-ray or CBCT scan to check your bone and plan the implant position precisely before anything is done.

  2. 2

    Implant placement

    The titanium post is placed into the jawbone under local anaesthesia. Most patients find it comparable to a tooth extraction — far more comfortable than they expected — and in selected cases a temporary tooth can go on the same day.

  3. 3

    Healing (osseointegration)

    Over roughly three to six months, the bone fuses to the post to form a stable root. You carry on normally during this time.

  4. 4

    The crown

    Once the implant is solid, we fit a custom crown (tooth cap) matched to your natural teeth, so the finished tooth blends in and bites like the rest.

After placement, most people return to their routine the next day, with mild soreness for a few days that ordinary pain relief handles. The fear that implant surgery is something to dread is mostly outdated — the infection or gap we're fixing is usually less comfortable than the procedure itself.

Missing several teeth or a full arch?

You don't need one implant for every missing tooth. With full-arch options such as All-on-4 and All-on-6, a complete fixed set of teeth is supported on just four to six implants per jaw — a stable, fixed alternative to loose dentures.

In suitable cases we can fit a fixed temporary set on the same day as surgery, with your final teeth fitted once healing is complete. Whether same-day fixed teeth suit you depends on your bone and bite, which we confirm from the scan — it isn't right for every patient, and we'll be straight with you about that. Implant, bridge or denture — an honest comparison

Replacing a missing tooth isn't one-size-fits-all, so here's the fair version.

OptionStrengthsTrade-offs
Implant The closest thing to a natural tooth. Stands on its own without touching the teeth either side, and it's the only option that protects the jawbone under the gap. Needs enough bone and a few months to heal.
Bridge Faster and surgery-free. The healthy teeth on each side are reshaped to carry it, and it doesn't stop the bone beneath the gap from shrinking.
Denture The lowest-cost, removable option. Less stable than the other two, and some people find it affects chewing and confidence.

At your consultation we'll tell you honestly which of these fits your case — and sometimes that's a bridge or denture, not an implant.

Frequently asked questions

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

The placement is done under local anaesthesia, so you won't feel pain during it — most patients compare it to having a tooth removed. Afterwards there's usually mild soreness for a few days that ordinary pain relief manages.

How long does the whole process take?

For a single implant, usually three to six months from placement to the final crown, because the bone needs time to fuse to the post. We give you your specific timeline after the scan.

How much does a single dental implant cost in Coimbatore?

As a guide, a single tooth implant typically ranges from about ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 depending on the implant brand and crown, with extra if bone grafting is needed. Your exact cost is confirmed at your consultation.

Can I get fixed teeth in a day?

In suitable cases, yes — full-arch options like All-on-4 can carry a fixed temporary set on the same day as surgery, with the final teeth fitted after healing. Whether you're a same-day candidate depends on your bone and bite.

I lost the tooth a long time ago — can I still get an implant?

Often, yes. The bone may have shrunk in the gap, but a bone graft or sinus lift can usually rebuild enough support. A 3D scan tells us exactly what's needed.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, the implant post can last 15 years or more, frequently a lifetime. The crown attached to it may need replacing over the years, as any restoration can.

Can I have an implant if I have diabetes or I smoke?

It's often still possible, but both can affect healing and success, so we assess your situation individually and are honest about the risks before recommending treatment.
